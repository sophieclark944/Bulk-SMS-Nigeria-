## What Is a Bulk SMS Service?

A **Bulk SMS Service** allows businesses to send messages to multiple mobile numbers through a centralized messaging platform. Instead of sending messages individually, organizations can manage larger communication campaigns from one system.

Businesses commonly use bulk SMS for:

- Promotional offers and product announcements
- OTP and account verification
- Order and payment notifications
- Appointment and booking reminders
- Customer service updates
- Important business announcements

This approach helps organizations manage regular customer communication more efficiently.

## Promotional, Transactional and OTP Messaging

Businesses generally use three important types of SMS communication:

**Promotional SMS:** Used for marketing campaigns, discounts, special offers, and product announcements.

**Transactional SMS:** Used for order updates, payment confirmations, account notifications, and service-related information.

**OTP SMS:** Used to deliver one-time passwords for login, registration, and account verification.

Choosing the appropriate message type helps organizations deliver relevant information to customers.
